Why a disagreement is not automatically a bug

Four of the eighteen expectations are unclear, and unclear is the hardest verdict to reach because it requires the judge to decline. When the clerk rules where the expectation says unclear, it is performing confidence the promise did not earn. That is the failure mode worth watching, and it is why the unclear share is reported next to the score.

What separates this from the published number

One model, one pass, in your browser. The published number is a validator committee that must agree, run three times, with the stability across runs reported alongside it. A good score here is encouraging and nothing more.
Integration

One wrapper, no rewrite

A selling endpoint keeps its handler and publishes a promise. A buying agent keeps its call and gains the ability to contest it. Neither side changes how the money moves.

Selling endpoint publishes a promise 
recourse.serve({
  promise: "Spot price for the requested pair,
    three venues, under five seconds.",
  price: "0.01 USDC",
  handler,
})

The promise is the only new thing the seller writes. It is what the judge will be handed, so vagueness costs the seller its own protection.

Buying agent gains a dispute right 
const res = await recourse.pay(url, params)

// the response arrived with no added latency
if (!res.satisfies(promise))
  await res.contest()

One call to contest, inside the hold window. The bond is sized to the cost of judgment, so a frivolous case is not free.
